### 🛠️ Open-Source Video-as-Code Engine

Check out [**`make-pictures/make-pictures-studio`**](https://github.com/make-pictures/make-pictures-studio) — our domain-agnostic `60 FPS` animation toolkit designed for **Google Antigravity** and **Claude Code**:
- 🎭 **Visual Kits (`src/kits/`):** 2.5D Paper Pop-Up Book (`paper-diorama-3d`), Stylized Isometric 3D (`isometric-3d`), Infinite 2D Motion Diagrams (`motion-diagrams-2d`), or your own custom style.
- 🎙️ **Voiceover & Lip-Sync Pipeline:** Automated ElevenLabs timestamp generation (`eleven_multilingual_v2`) + overdamped `60 FPS` posture springs (`speakingWeight`).
- 📐 **Horizontal & Vertical:** Render full `16:9` episodes (`1920×1080`) and `9:16` Shorts (`1080×1920`) from the same declarative codebase.
